§5 — Conclusions & Recommendations

Key Findings

  • Interruption: TOR=97.0% (excellent — near-Moshi level). GPT rating=3.96/5. Latency=339ms.
  • Pause Candor: TOR=70.4% — model interrupts mid-sentence pauses 70% of the time (best-in-class is Gemini Live 25.5%).
  • Pause Synthetic: TOR=86.9% — even worse on synthetic pauses; model appears to rely heavily on prosodic cues absent in TTS audio.
  • Turn-Taking: TOR=84.0%, latency=307ms — good responsiveness at turn boundaries.

Recommendations

  • Pause handling is the primary weakness: TOR ≥ 70% on both Candor and Synthetic data. Train with more mid-sentence pause examples and negative rewards for interrupting pauses.
  • Interruption handling is already strong (TOR=97%, rating≈4.0) — maintain this capability while improving pause discrimination.
  • Turn-taking latency (307ms) is competitive — minor improvement possible toward dGSLM's 265ms target.
